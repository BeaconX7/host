MINECRAFT HOSTING FAQ

Everything you need to know about setting up, migrating, and managing your Minecraft world on ShadowNode.

How do I purchase a Minecraft plan?
Simply click "Join to Buy" on any plan to join our official Discord server. Our automated ticket system and team will instantly assist you with plan activation, custom billing, and control panel setup.
Can players on Minecraft Bedrock and Java play together?
Yes! All ShadowNode servers support GeyserMC and Floodgate plugins with 1-click installation from our control panel, allowing players on PC, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, iOS, and Android to join the same world seamlessly.
Can I install custom modpacks or my own custom Jar files?
Absolutely. Our panel gives you full SFTP and Web File Manager access. You can upload custom server Jars (Paper, Purpur, Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Spigot) and configure custom Java launch flags or mod configurations without restrictions.
What happens if I need to upgrade my RAM later?
Upgrading is completely seamless. You can scale your RAM, CPU allocation, or NVMe storage anytime without changing your server IP, port, or world files.
What is your refund guarantee for Minecraft servers?
We provide a transparent 24 to 48-hour refund guarantee. If our infrastructure fails to deliver the promised performance or reliability on your initial activation, simply reach out to support on Discord for a prompt refund.
