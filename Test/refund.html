REFUND POLICY & SLA

We back our infrastructure with a fair 24 to 48-hour satisfaction guarantee and proactive SLA credits.

1. The 24-48 Hour Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to test our servers with total confidence. If our infrastructure fails to meet your performance standards or expectations within 24 to 48 hours of your initial order, you may request a full refund of your subscription fee.

2. Eligibility & Exclusions

To prevent abuse of our compute resources, refund eligibility is governed by the following criteria:

  • Eligible on initial orders for new services. Renewals of existing active services are non-refundable.
  • Accounts found in violation of our Terms of Service (e.g. running DDoS scripts, port scanners, malware, or unlicensed crypto mining) forfeit all refund claims.
  • Custom enterprise quote hardware or custom domain registrations cannot be refunded once provisioned from upstream providers.

3. 99.9% Uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA)

ShadowNode guarantees a minimum 99.9% monthly uptime across all host nodes and networking switches. If an unscheduled outage caused by our hardware, hypervisor, or network drops availability below 99.9% within any billing cycle, you are eligible for account SLA credits proportional to the downtime duration.

4. How to Request a Refund

Requesting a refund is simple and hassle-free:

  1. Contact our team via Discord or our Support Center within 48 hours of initial order placement.
  2. Provide your registered email address and Server ID.
  3. Our billing staff will verify the request and issue the refund back to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
