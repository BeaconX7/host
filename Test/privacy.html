PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026. How we collect, safeguard, and respect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver, provision, and maintain your hosting account, including:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, Discord handle (optional).
  • Billing details: Transaction records and payment confirmation tokens (processed securely via PCI-compliant payment gateways; we never store raw credit card numbers).
  • Technical session logs: IP addresses, browser user-agents, and panel login timestamps for security and abuse prevention.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your data is strictly utilized to:

  • Automatically provision and configure your game servers or VPS containers.
  • Deliver transactional notifications, service maintenance alerts, and billing invoices.
  • Prevent fraudulent registrations and protect our network against malicious attacks.
  • Provide personalized customer support via Discord or email when requested.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

ShadowNode never sells, rents, or monetizes client personal data. We only share information with vetted infrastructure partners (such as upstream tier-1 transit carriers and payment processors) strictly as required to operate your services.

4. Server Data & Confidentiality

Your server files, worlds, plugins, databases, and configuration settings are confidential. ShadowNode staff will never inspect, copy, or distribute your private server files unless explicitly requested by you for technical troubleshooting or compelled by legitimate legal process.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Upon account termination or cancellation, server files are queued for automated permanent destruction in accordance with our data lifecycle schedules. Clients may request full account deletion by contacting support.
