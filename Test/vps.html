Questions & Answers

VPS HOSTING FAQ

Technical details, networking questions, and resource allocation policies.

How do I purchase a VPS plan?
Simply click "Join to Buy" on your preferred Intel or AMD plan to join our official Discord server. Our team and ticket bot will instantly guide you through node allocation, OS selection, and access details.
Do I receive full root access with my VPS?
Yes, you receive complete root access via SSH on Linux and Administrator access via VNC. You have total freedom to install custom software packages, run Docker, configure web servers, and manage firewalls.
What is the difference between Intel and AMD VPS?
Intel VPS provides solid, dependable multi-core performance for background services, databases, and microservices. AMD VPS delivers higher single-thread IPC and multithread bandwidth, ideal for high-tick game servers and heavy compute workloads.
How are DDoS attacks handled on VPS instances?
All VPS ports pass through our upstream 3.2+ Tbps automated scrubbing pipeline. Volumetric Layer 3 and Layer 4 attacks (SYN floods, UDP amplification) are dropped within milliseconds without taking down your virtual machine.
Can I reinstall or format my VPS anytime?
Yes. The control panel includes 1-click operating system reinstallations, instant reboot triggers, recovery mode, and web VNC access in case you lock yourself out of SSH.
